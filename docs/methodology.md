## Conceptual Framework

Urban inequality is treated as a **network-mediated spatial process**.
Opportunity is defined not by availability but by **minimum travel time**.

---

## Network Modeling

- Nodes represent road intersections
- Edges represent road segments
- Edge weights correspond to travel time

Shortest-path algorithms compute realistic accessibility.

---

## Index Construction

Indicators are standardized and aggregated using
**Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** to ensure:

- objective weighting
- variance maximization
- avoidance of subjective index design

---

## Spatial Validation

- **Global Moran’s I** tests overall spatial dependence
- **Local Moran’s I (LISA)** identifies clusters and outliers

This distinguishes random variation from structural inequality.
